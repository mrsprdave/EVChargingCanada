Research and Verification

Human Researched, Directly Verified.

EV Charging Canada is an independent, non-AI resource created to give Canadian EV drivers clear, accurate details on

  • Home EV charging reward programs without marketing clutter 
  • Electrical Code/installation

Details on this site are gathered using various methods and tools. Every detail is personally reviewed for accuracy and applicability, using hands-on experience and background knowledge.

  • Primary sources: Official documentation and terms are reviewed.
  • Direct program outreach: Individual programs are contacted to verify the information contained within and clarify vague or missing important details. While we make attempts to do such, not all programs are responsive.
  • Secondary sources and community insights: We monitor other sources of information, including comments in EV communities, to ensure the information is collaborated as much as possible. 

Ongoing Audits: We are continually re-verifying the various program details, with dates listed.
