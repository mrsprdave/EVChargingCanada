ChargeLab Rewards

Eligibility

Single-family residence in Canada

Rate

10¢ per kWh

Payout

Quarterly by gift card (switching to cash payouts for rewards by the end of 2026)

Minimum

The minimum rewards payout amount is CA $5.00. If the amount accrued in a given quarter is less than $5.00, there will be no reward payout for the quarter.

Chargers (EVSEs)

Bring your own OCPP smart charger

Compatible options include:

  • Autel Home Charger
  • EVDuty
  • Wallbox Pulsar
  • IYILO
  • Fractal
  • Siemens VersiCharge
  • SWTCH/Home EVL007
  • Grizzl-E Smart (may require unlocking fee)

Functionality

  • Uses ChargeLab app, unless the charger supports dual connections
