Charging Right

*Charging Right has not responded to our request for further information, so information is limited.

Eligibility

Ontario pilot program

Reward Rate

3¢ to 16¢ per kWh, depending on plan and tier.

  • Freemium: Starts at 3¢ per kWh, +1¢ per extra 1,000 eligible kWh up to 10¢ per kWh
  • Premium: Starts at 10¢ per kWh, +1¢ per extra 1,000 eligible kWh up to 13¢ per kWh
  • Premium Plus: Starts at 13¢ per kWh, +1¢ per extra 1,000 eligible kWh up to 16¢ per kWh

Payout

Annual
