Chargy

Eligibility

Residents in BC, MB, ON, QC, NL and PEI. 

Rate

10¢ per kWh

Limited Time: Extra 5¢ per kWh for 6 months (offer expires Sept. 30, 2026) 

Payout

Paid monthly by e-transfer or direct deposit.

Minimum

Minimum 100 kWh per month to receive payment (no payment if below 100 kWh as they need to cover operation costs)

Chargers (EVSEs)

Bring your own eligible OCPP charger, or purchase a Wallbox from Chargy.

Eligible Chargers:

  • Autel MaxiCharger Home
  • Wallbox Pulsar Plus
  • Elmec EV Duty
  • Lite On
  • Joint Tech
  • Grizzl-E Smart (may require paying Grizzl-E to unlock)
  • And more

Not Tesla, ChargePoint, Flo, Pion, or locked Grizzl-E.

Chargers offered to purchase: $250 cash bonus on charger purchase – after 12 months (you always own the charger).

  • Wallbox Pulsar Plus 40A: J1772 or NACS - $849
  • Wallbox Pulsar Plus 48A: J1772 or NACS - $899

$50 Flat rate shipping

Functionality

Uses software from "Eddie" configured for plug-and-charge, meaning no app is required, but user dashboard is "coming soon"

Promotions

  • $25 Cash Bonus with Referral (after 3 months)
You may use "EVChargingCanada" in the "hear about us" box to get the bonus.
  • Extra $0.05 per kWh for 6 months (offer expires Sept. 30, 2026) 
  • $250 cash bonus on charger purchase – after 12 months
