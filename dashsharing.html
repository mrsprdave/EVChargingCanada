DashSharing

Eligibility

Across Canada

Rate

$0.11 per kWh after deposit refund ($0.03 before 1500 kWh)  

Payout

Once balance reaches $100 (1st of next month). 

Minimum

No particular minimum requirement

Chargers

DashSharing charger required - free with deposit.

Standard Charger:
40A EVSE, 18ft cable, hardwired or plug in, J1772 or NACS
$300 deposit (with discounts)

Ultra Charger:
48A EVSE, 18ft cable, hardwired J1772 or NACS
$449 deposit (with discounts)

$30 shipping

25ft cable option to come late 2026

Launch Promo: $100 off deposit with code CD100

Additional $50 off deposit with code EVCHARGINGCANADA

(both codes should work together in checkout)

Functionality

  • Charger is Level 2 (240V) only (no Level 1)
  • Has capability of current derating for electrical service capacity
  • App has scheduled charging (e.g. for TOU rates)

Terms of note:

Deposit refunded after 1500 kWh and Terms fulfilled.

License to use DashSharing charger. Title remains with Dashsharing.

If participation in the program is terminated:

  • Before 1500 kWh: The charger must be returned. (unreturned chargers incur replacement fee)
  • After 1500 kWh: You can keep the charger - A $50 buyout fee permanently transfers full ownership.

Must be your primary charging device, and remain continuously connected to a Wi-Fi network (limited offline charging).
Must be installed installed in compliance with all relevant safety standards and electrical codes (not necessarily requiring an Electrician, although I'll add that using an Electrician is recommended)
