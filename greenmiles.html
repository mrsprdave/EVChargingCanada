GreenMiles

(by Pion Power)

Eligibility

Across Canada

Rate

10¢ per kWh + Top-up bonuses

Payout

Annual in March of the following year.

Option of early redemption for store credit.

Minimum

No particular minimum requirement (GreenMiles are redeemed in $100 increments though).

Chargers

Pion Power Flex-AC or Flex-AC Elite charger required. (Flex-AC LE does not qualify)

Flex-AC has been discontinued, but if you already have it or can buy it second-hand, it works for GreenMiles. Came in 32A and 40A options. 

Flex-AC Elite is the new generation with Bluetooth (works better for setup). Comes in 32A and 40A, J1772 and now NACS options. 

  • Plug-in with 5-15P (120V, level 1) and 14-50P (240V, level 2) adapters. (no hardwired)

Typically go on sale, with the last (Labour Day) sale being:

  • $299.99 for Flex AC Elite 32A
  • $349.99 for Flex AC Elite 40A (J1772 or NACS pre-order) 

Less discount codes available (like GREENDPION10)

Functionality

  • Level 1 and Level 2 capable, and both earn GreenMiles
  • App has scheduled charging and can adjust the current
  • No physical switches to derate for Electrical Code purposes

Terms of note:

1 GreenMile per kWh. When 1,000 GreenMiles is reached, it is converted into $100 in redeemable rewards, which can be redeemed early for store credit or in March by cheque.

Promotions

Discount codes on charger purchase (both work together):

  • GREENDPION10
  • WELCOMEPION5

Top-up bonus (April 1st to December 31st, 2026)

 For the following level of points accumulated you earn:

  • 1000 pts: Flex-AC Silicone Case
  • 2000 pts: 5-PC Insulated Tool Set
  • 4000 pts: P201 Portable Power Station
  • 8000 pts: $300 Store Credit

The top-up tiers are cumulative - E.g. if someone reaches 8000 points, they receive the rewards from all (4) tiers, not just the highest. 

Thanksgiving Promo (Oct. 1st to Dec. 31st 2026)

16¢ per kWh after 5000 kWh

Welcome Bonus 20 points ($2) when you register for GreenMiles.

200 bonus points ($20) when you sign up using a referral code

  • Feel free to use the referral code EVCHARGINGCANADA

Note: the discount codes and referral code are separate items. The discount codes are used for the purchase of the charger during checkout. The referral code is used after receiving and setting up the charger, when you go to activate GreenMiles in the app.
