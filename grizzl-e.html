Grizzl-E Club

Eligibility

Canada.

Reward Rate

Essentials Club: Starts at $0.03 per kWh with complimentary charger (pay shipping, plus $100 deposit that's refunded after first charge)

Premium Club: Starts at $0.05 per kWh with purchased charger

$0.01 increase every 5,000 kWh charged up to $0.10 kWh

+ $0.05 Thanksgiving 2026 bonus, for purchased chargers

Payout

Monthly with $10 minimum

Minimum

4-6 charging sessions per month

Chargers

Compatible Grizzl-E charger required.

Essentials Club: Offers the following free/complimentary chargers. Pay for shipping (depending on location) and $100 deposit that's refunded after first charge.

  • Grizzl-E Classic Connect 40A
  • Grizzl-E Mini Connect 40A
  • Grizzl-E Ultimate 48A

Premium Club: Purchased chargers

  • Grizzl-E Duo Connect 40A, $1199.99
  • Grizzl-E Classic Connect 40A, $499.99
  • Grizzl-E Ultimate 48A, $649.99
  • Grizzl-E Ultimate 80A, $1199
  • Grizzl-E Mini Connect 40A, $649.99

Terms of note:

  • If the membership is terminated (by member or by United Chargers for any reason) the charger shall be returned to United Chargers at your own expense.
  • Rewards start at $0.03/$0.05 per kWh, with $0.01 increase every 5,000 kWh charged up to $0.10 kWh
  • Must be professionally installed by a licensed electrician (except pre-existing 14-50 receptacle)
  • A $50 cancellation fee applies to cancelled or returned orders, starting from when the order is placed (not shipped)
  • There is a $600 OCPP Unlock fee, to enable OCPP on purchased chargers (OCPP doesn't apply to complimentary chargers)
