MetroEV Rewards

Eligibility

Across Canada: Homeowners & Renters, Condo Unit Owners, Condo Corporations, Commercial & Business Properties.

Reward Rate

10¢ per kWh

Payout

Annual. By direct deposit after the applicable credits have been processed.

Minimum

Annual earnings must reach at least $100 before a payout is issued. If earnings remain below $100 for two consecutive calendar years, the balance is forfeited.

Chargers

Buy from metroEV or use eligible third-party OCPP chargers.

Terms of note

  • If your accrued earnings remain below $100.00 CAD for two (2) consecutive calendar years, the entire accrued balance will be forfeited.
  • Neither party may cancel this Agreement without cause during the first twelve (12) months following your enrollment date (the "Minimum Term").
  • As a non-binding reference, a Charger delivering fewer than approximately 1,700–2,500 kWh per year may not cover metroEV’s per-install costs.
