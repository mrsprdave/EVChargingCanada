Reward Program Summary

"Select" programs (default) are reward programs selected due to having more history and competitive rates.

**Charger Definitions

  • OCPP: Third-party chargers that are OCPP compatible are eligible.
  • Provided: Charger provided "free", but typically deposit and shipping applies.
  • Purchase: Charger purchased from program (own brand). 
  • Tesla: Tesla Wall Connector

*Notes

Charging Right: Multiple tiers - rate increases each 1000 kWh. Freemium: 3¢ to 10¢; Premium: 10¢ to 13¢; Premium Plus: 13¢ to 16¢

Chargy: Provinces are BC, MB, ON, QC, NL and PEI

GreenMiles: Thanksgiving promotion - 16¢ after 5000 kWh (Oct. 1 to Dec. 31, 2026)

Grizzl-E Club (Essentials and Premium): Multiple tiers - rate increases each 5000 kWh, up to 10¢. Thanksgiving bonus - additional 5¢ on purchased chargers for one year.

Roulez Electrique: Rate applies to standard chargers in the provinces: QC, ON, BC, MB, NL. FractalEV and Sigenergy chargers have higher rates, and other provinces have lower rates. See program page for details.
