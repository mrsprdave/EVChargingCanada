BYOC - Bring Your Own Charger

Tesla WC* - Tesla Wall Connector - Rewards from Tesla chargers are less promised.

The reason being, there is no agreement between Tesla and the reward programs regarding the use of charging data for carbon credits. Theoretically, Tesla could try to claim the credits for themselves, and create a conflict in data. As such, there’s still a risk that the credits aren’t successful and nobody gets the rewards for those particular chargers.

There is also the issue of double counting. Each charging session can only be counted once for the purpose of carbon credits. There are multiple reward programs that use the Tesla WC. For example, programs state that the same charger being enrolled in different programs within the same calendar year creates a data violation and risk disqualification. 

Saying that, if you already have a Tesla Wall Connector and don’t want to change, this is still an option to potentially get rewards. Just be aware of the risk. This risk essentially applies to all programs that use the Tesla chargers.
