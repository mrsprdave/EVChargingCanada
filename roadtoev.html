RoadToEV

Carbon Credit Program

Eligibility

Across Canada

Rate

10¢ per kWh

April 1 – June 30, 2026 charging can be backdated at 5¢ per kWh.

Payout

Annual around July 1st for the previous year, paid as a one-time credit to your RoadToEV wallet and can be withdrawn using e-Transfer.

Minimum

$20 minimum balance required in Carbon Credits to request an annual payout.

Chargers

Tesla Gen3 Wall Connector only (currently)

Notes

Option of keeping charger private, or making public for community charging (via app).

This uses the third-party Tesla charger, concerns regarding Tesla apply to this program (such as double counting and Tesla cooperating).

Promotions

Referral program:

  • $10 in Volt points for signing up (promotional credit used in the RoadToEV platform)
  • $20 in "Carbon Credits" for successfully connecting an eligible Tesla Wall Connector

To receive the above bonuses use: Invite/Sign up link 

https://www.roadtoev.com/carbon-credits-program
