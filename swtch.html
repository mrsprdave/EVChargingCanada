SWTCH Home Charging

Eligibility

Resident of Canada

Rate

11¢ per kWh after 1500 kWh (3¢ for first 1500 kWh) 

Payout

Once balance reaches $100

Minimum

No particular minimum requirement

Chargers

SWTCH charger required - free with deposit.

Plug in or hardwired, J1772 or NACS options, 25ft cable

$300 deposit for charger, refunded after 1500 kWh

$30 shipping

Functionality

  • Charger is Level 2 (240V) only (no Level 1)
  • Limited app functions: No scheduled charging or current adjustment in the app
  • Charger can be derated for limited electrical service

Terms of note:

  • $75 restocking fee (plus shipping) if charger is returned before the deposit refund.
  • Deposit refunded after 1500 kWh
  • One free charger per household - additional chargers can be purchased

Promotions

  • CAA Atlantic members can earn an additional $0.01 per kWh
