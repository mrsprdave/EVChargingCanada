WattsApp

Eligibility

Canadian Residents

Rate

3¢ per kWh (base rate) + various bonuses and contests

Payout

Annual once the carbon credits are verified and approved in Q1 the following year.

Minimum

Minimum 10,000 points or $100 for payout.

Chargers

Tesla Wall Connector or Universal Wall Connector (Gen 3).

Terms of note

  • Tesla vehicle also required
  • Charging points: 3 points per kWh (100 points = $1)
  • Minimum 10,000 points or $100 for payout.
  • This uses the third-party Tesla charger, concerns regarding Tesla apply to this program (such as double counting and Tesla cooperating).

Promotions

Triple Play Program

+3 points/kWh bonus
• Monthly +12 points/kWh giveaway
• Quarterly giveaway to double your Charging Points balance
• Winter Supercharging rebate – up to $75
• Monthly Tesla Wall Connector giveaway
• Annual Tesla Model Y RWD giveaway

With the Triple Play Program, members can potentially get up to 18 points/$0.18 per kWh (if successful on giveaway) and winter supercharging rebates, or enter into other giveaways.
